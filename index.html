How it works

Three steps. No guesswork.

GoalBuddy is built around what you're trying to achieve, not a swipe deck of strangers.

1

Set a Goal

Create a Goal Card — what you're working on, how often, and what kind of buddy you're after. Up to three at once.

2

Find your match

GoalBuddy shows you the strongest compatible matches for that specific goal — people actually working toward the same thing.

3

Achieve together

Send Interested, get accepted, and start a real conversation. No unrestricted messaging until you're both in.

Not a dating app. Not a social feed.

GoalBuddy exists for one reason: to help you find someone real to work toward a goal with. That shapes everything about how it's built.

No hearts. No hot-or-not. No infinite scroll of strangers.

✓Matching led by shared goals, not photos
✓You review a real profile before you connect
✓No messaging until both people say yes
✓Report and block built in from day one
Why we're building this

Digital tools that get people offline, together

GoalBuddy is built by Luxford Interactive. We believe technology should help level the playing field, help people grow, and — a little paradoxically for a software company — get people off their phones and doing something real, together.

⚖

Reducing inequality

Everyone deserves the same shot at finding the right people to grow with, not just those with the biggest existing network.

🎓

Learning & growth

Especially for young people building skills, habits, and careers — progress is easier with someone alongside you.

🌍

Real-world community

The goal of the app is to get you off the app — meeting up, showing up, and actually doing the thing.

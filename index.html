Two ways to find your goal partner.

Whether it's a long-term goal or a one-off plan, it starts with the thing you want to do.

Goal mode
For things you want to stick with.

Find a buddy for the goals that need consistency: getting fitter, studying regularly, learning a language, eating better, or building a new habit.

Activity mode
For things you want to do.

Find someone for the plan in front of you: a walk, a tennis match, a gym session, a concert, a coffee, a hike, or a weekend activity.

Long-term goal or one-off plan, the idea is the same: find someone who wants in.

How it works

1

Post what you're looking for

"Be my gym buddy." "Join me for tennis." "Study with me on Thursdays." Keep it clear, local, and specific.

2

Find people who want the same thing

Browse by activity, goal, location, availability, and level.

3

Wave, match, then chat

Send a short intro. If they accept, you can chat and make the plan.

Built for doing, not dating.

GoalBuddy isn't about endless swiping. It's about finding someone with the same intention: to show up, take part, practise, train, learn, try something new, or simply get out and do the thing.

✓Goals and activities first, photos second
✓Intentional connections, not endless browsing
✓Meet to do something real, not just keep chatting
✓Both people opt in before the conversation starts
✓Simple tools to block, report, and stay in control

No swipes. No mixed signals. No scrolling for the sake of scrolling. Just people finding people to do real things with.

You decide who to contact, who to accept, and when to step away. GoalBuddy is designed around mutual interest, clear intent, and real-world respect.
